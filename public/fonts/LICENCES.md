# Fonts

Self-hosted so the site makes no third-party requests. All three are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), which allows bundling and redistribution with the site.

| File | Family | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shippori-mincho-500.woff2` | Shippori Mincho, weight 500, subset to Latin-1, punctuation and 春夏秋冬庭 | FONTDASU | https://github.com/fontdasu/ShipporiMincho |
| `instrument-sans.woff2` | Instrument Sans, variable weight 400–700, Latin subset | Instrument | https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans |
| `ibm-plex-mono-400.woff2` | IBM Plex Mono, weight 400, Latin subset | IBM | https://github.com/IBM/plex |

The woff2 subsets were taken from Google Fonts' builds of these families.
